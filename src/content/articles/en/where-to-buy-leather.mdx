As a leathercrafter, you will soon wonder where to find good leather, near you or online. Before anything else, look for a tannery or a leather shop nearby: touching the leather and asking a professional for advice is the best way to choose. If, like me, you have none around, here are the online shops I buy from.

## Your leather must be strong and durable

People who buy leather goods expect sturdy, long-lasting pieces. Your hides should live up to that, and respect nature, even though they come from animals.

That is why I always look for vegetable-tanned leathers with a respectful background, for animals and for people, like the ones from the [Genuine Vegetable-Tanned Leather Consortium](https://www.pellealvegetale.it/en/sustainability/). I explain what it commits to in [why I use Tuscan vegetable-tanned leather](/guides/tuscan-vegetable-tanned-leather/).

### Decocuir

I discovered [Decocuir](https://bit.ly/decocuirfr) thanks to a French crafter when I started. I ordered several small squares of leather to practise and to find out which thickness and type of leather I liked working with.

Today, I mostly order glue, Tokonole, straps for belts and small hardware like buckles there, because these items are good quality.
