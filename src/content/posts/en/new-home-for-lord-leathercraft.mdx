Until now, lord.crofte.fr was a simple page of links. It is now the home of everything that does not fit in a shop: guides, materials, tools and the stories behind the pieces.

## What you will find here

- **[Guides](/guides/)**: in-depth tutorials, from caring for leather to how a pattern is designed.
- **[My materials](/materials/)**: the leathers, threads and buckles I work with, with where to find them.
- **[My tools](/tools/)**: from a beginner’s kit to professional machines, sorted by level.
- **[Portfolio](/portfolio/)**: the pieces I have made over the years, with their leathers and dimensions.
- **The blog**, where you are now: shorter posts, news from the workbench, answers to your questions and quick tips.

## What does not change

The patterns are still sold on the [shop](https://shop.crofte.fr), as instant PDF downloads. This site links to them whenever a guide or a post is related to a pattern.

The site sets no cookies and does not track you. It is available in English and French: use the language switch at the top of each page.

If you have a question you would like to see answered here, ask me on [Instagram](https://www.instagram.com/lordleathercraft/).
