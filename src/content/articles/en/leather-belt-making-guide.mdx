## Which leather for a belt?

You want your belt to last and not stretch, while staying comfortable to wear. I recommend a leather **3 to 4 mm thick**, with a **medium to firm temper**. For a two-tone belt, glue two pieces of 1.5 to 2 mm. Need to convert? Here is a [mm, oz and inch converter](https://www.leffler.com.au/leather-thickness-conversion-chart/).

To choose the quality, read [types of leather explained](/guides/types-of-leather/). And for all my suppliers, see [where to buy quality leather](/guides/where-to-buy-leather/).

<details>
  <summary>Do I need every tool on this page to make a belt?</summary>

No, absolutely not. To try, focus on the first list, and only that one: good tools for their price. If you make more belts later, come back for the other lists. They cost more, but you will work faster and more accurately.

</details>
