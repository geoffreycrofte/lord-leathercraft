## Which leather should I use?

On a budget, buy a single thickness, around **1.2 or 1.4 mm**. The easiest to work with, in my opinion, is a **vegetable-tanned leather, medium to firm**. To understand why, read [types of leather explained](/guides/types-of-leather/).

Look for shops near you first: local sellers usually give the best advice. Otherwise, I am very happy with my two suppliers and partners:

- [Buy Leather Online](https://buyleatheronline.com/en/): a wide choice, and they split leather and sell half hides for free. Use the code **LORD7**.
- [Leatherbox](https://www.leatherbox.com/?sca_ref=4545380.cSRXJjRrgN): leather panels in several thicknesses, a good way to try different leathers. Use the code **LORD10**.

## Questions about wallets and cardholders

<details>
  <summary>What is the best pattern to start leathercraft?</summary>

Not easy to answer. Have a look at my [belt making guide](/guides/leather-belt-making-guide/): a belt takes far fewer steps. Otherwise, I recommend two of my patterns:

- [Nest](https://shop.crofte.fr/b/nest-multipurpose-cardholder-leather-wallet-template), the multipurpose one: it has several variations, and one of them should suit you.
- [Hug](https://shop.crofte.fr/b/minimalist-leather-cardholder-hug-pattern): small and quick to make. Make it a few times perfectly, and you will master almost every step.

Once you can make the Nest, you are ready for the whole [wallet collection](https://shop.crofte.fr/collection/leather-wallet-patterns).

</details>
